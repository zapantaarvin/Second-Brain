# DSC 385 — Data Exploration & Visualization

Same course number as [[DSC 385 - Principles of Data Science]] but a distinct folder/topic focus — likely a different section or semester offering; worth confirming which when this note is next touched. Coursework centers on reproducible reporting (Quarto `.qmd` files rendered to PDF/HTML/typst/tex) and data visualization critique using published figures as source material.

## Topics
- 6 homeworks (HW1–HW6) plus a course project, all built as Quarto documents
- Visualization critique using real published figures (Cheng et al. 2005, Graser et al. 2025, Morehead et al. 2025, Smaruj et al. 2025, Triandafillou et al. 2020, Wu et al. 2024)
- Project 1 has a rubric, solution, and a classmate's submission included (likely a peer-review exercise)

## Materials in the repo
Syllabus, 6 HW sets (each with `.qmd` source + multiple rendered formats), project 1 (instructions, rubric, solution, sample submission), olympics dataset.
