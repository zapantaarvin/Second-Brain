# Protein Bar Business — "FFN" (Filipino Functional Nutrition)
> Working brand name: **FFN — Filipino Functional Nutrition**. Per Arvin (2026-08-16), the brand isn't finalized yet — FFN is the current working name used throughout the blueprint docs, not a locked-in decision.
> Placeholder name in [[Kanban Business Hub]]: **"Pilipinas Fuel"** (per Arvin, 2026-08-16 — this is also just a placeholder, not the real brand).

A faith-driven Filipino nutrition brand — protein bars and related products bringing authentic Filipino flavors to the US market.

## Mission & identity
"A team of Godly men who love Christ, committed to doing business the right way — with integrity, generosity, and a heart for Filipino communities." Five brand pillars: Nourished by Culture, Fueled by Purpose, Made with Integrity, Built for Community, Grounded in Faith. A portion of every sale is earmarked for the Filipino community (scholarships, small-business support, nonprofits).

## Market targets (as stated in the blueprint)
- $163B+ Filipino-American buying power
- 8.3% CAGR for the functional food market
- Positioned as the first Filipino-flavored functional bar brand in the US market
- $1M+ revenue target within 12–24 months

## Product line
Ube Coconut Bar, Pandan Protein Bar, Turon Cinnamon Bar, Salted Bibingka Bar, Protein Shake, Protein Powder.

## Production process (4 steps, as planned)
1. **Rent a food-grade kitchen** — licensed shared-use kitchen (CloudKitchens, Kitchen United, local commissary), FDA food facility registration, ServSafe certification, liability insurance
2. **Sample batching** — 3 core flavors, documented ratios/sourcing, 25–50 unit trial batches, per-unit cost tracking
3. **Community evaluation** — 2–3 tasting events at Filipino community events/churches, 50+ evaluators, 3+ iteration rounds
4. **Lab testing** — certified lab (Eurofins/SGS/Covance) for nutrition panel, allergen testing, shelf-life stability — required for FDA label compliance

## Packaging
Design via Canva AI or a Filipino designer (Upwork); FDA-compliant nutrition label + UPC (GS1 US, $250 one-time); prototype testing before full print run; eco-conscious (compostable/recyclable) wrappers tied to the brand's stewardship values; scale to a co-packer (PartnerSlate/YouBar/EMPWR) at 1,000+ units/month.
