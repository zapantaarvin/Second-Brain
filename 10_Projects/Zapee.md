Research and planning for a China-to-Philippines import + e-commerce business, starting on Shopee with a limited-capital (₱500,000), test-and-scale approach. The name "Zapee" actually already appeared in the original research repo (one doc was titled "ZAPEE Ventures — Import & E-Commerce Blueprint") before it became the team's official pick.

## Source material

### GitHub repo — `Philippines-E-commerce` (research/planning)

**1. Alibaba Import Business Plan (Carousel)** — general framework for launching a lightweight-goods import business with limited capital:
- Budget allocation across legal setup, inventory, operations, contingencies
- Product selection: "small, lightweight, useful, and repeatable"
- Supplier vetting and sampling protocols
- Staged marketplace expansion: Shopee → TikTok Shop → branded site
- Weekly operating rhythm for reorder decisions
- Risk mitigation via rapid testing and fast elimination of underperforming SKUs

**2. ZAPEE Ventures — Import & E-Commerce Blueprint** — Philippines-regulatory-focused blueprint for personal care/household product imports:
- Five-phase roadmap: supplier qualification → online sales
- Philippine customs duty calculations, trade agreement optimization
- **FDA permitting takes 4–8 weeks — must start Day 1**, or shipments get held at port accumulating storage fees
- Platform comparison: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop — distinct buyer segments per platform
- 24-item pre-launch checklist: registrations, permitting, logistics, platform setup, financials
- **Key risk:** undervaluation penalties and product seizure if customs/FDA compliance isn't sequenced correctly
