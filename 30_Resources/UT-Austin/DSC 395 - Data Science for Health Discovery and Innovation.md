# DSC 395 — Data Science for Health Discovery and Innovation

Applies data science/R methods to clinical/health datasets (brain cancer, heart, bone marrow transplant, lung, hepatocellular, pancreatic) — survival analysis and clinical prediction modeling based on dataset naming.

## Structure
- 4 lecture note parts (PDF) + ~14 numbered lecture-video companion R scripts
- 2 midterms (each with 3–4 variants: a/b/c/d) + 1 final-style exam-2 (4 variants)
- 6 homeworks with real clinical datasets

## Materials in the repo
Syllabus, lecture notes (parts 1–4), lecture video R scripts, midterm variants, exam 2 variants, HW2–HW6 with datasets (brain cancer, heart, BMT, lung, hepatocellular, pancreatic).
