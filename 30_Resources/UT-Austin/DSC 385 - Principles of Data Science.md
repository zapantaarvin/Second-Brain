# DSC 385 — Principles of Data Science

Spring 2025. Weekly-structured course (week 4 through week 12) covering the standard tidyverse/R data science pipeline, plus 4 course projects with formal reports.

## Topics (by week)
- Week 4: Tidyverse intro, data transformation, visual summaries
- Week 6: Dates/times, joining, basic data structures
- Week 7: Linear regression (inference, diagnostics, prediction), logistic regression
- Week 9–10: Tidymodels (1 & 2), nonparametric methods, prediction metrics
- Week 12: Shiny apps (dataset explorer, scatterplot, lecture examples)

## Projects
4 projects with instructions + submitted reports (Project 1 has an additional formal report; Project 2 has draft + submission versions).

## Materials in the repo
Syllabus, weekly slides/R scripts/PDFs, 4 project instruction sets + reports, datasets (Spotify/YouTube, planets), lab 0.
